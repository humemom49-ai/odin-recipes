biryani recipe

Description

Biryani is a fragrant, spiced rice dish layered with tender meat, vegetables, or eggs.

It is slow-cooked to perfection with aromatic herbs, saffron, and caramelized onions for a rich, festive flavor

ingredients

  • Basmati rice and meat or vegetables
  • Yogurt, onions, and ginger-garlic paste
  • Whole spices, saffron, and fresh herbs

Steps

  1. Marinate meat or vegetables in yogurt and spices
  2. Parboil basmati rice until mostly cooked
  3. Layer them together and steam on low heat
