burger recipe

Description

A burger is a cooked patty placed inside a sliced bun.

It is topped with cheese, fresh veggies, and sauces.

ingredients

  • Burger buns and a beef or chicken patty
  • Cheese slice, lettuce, and tomato
  • Onions, pickles, and burger sauce

Steps

  1. Season and cook the patty until done.
  2. Toast the buns and melt cheese on the patty.
  3. Layer the patty, veggies, and sauce inside the buns.
